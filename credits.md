## Knowledge and community

- The grain profiles, recipes and origin stories synthesize publicly available
  agricultural and culinary research and the lived knowledge of farming and
  cooking communities across West, East, Southern and Horn of Africa and the
  diaspora. Where a dish carries a community name (jollof, injera, uji, fura,
  thiakry, okpa, zobo, mchicha), the credit belongs to those kitchens first.
- Producer entries are research based directory descriptions, not endorsements.
  Cooperatives, women's groups and processors named or described here do the
  actual work; the credit is theirs.
